**Pixel Pilferer: Rebound** is developed with a strict **Open-Source Asset-First, Zero-AI Asset Policy**. All visual sprites, tilesets, animations, sound effects, music tracks, and fonts are authentic human creations released under permissive public domain and open-source licenses.
